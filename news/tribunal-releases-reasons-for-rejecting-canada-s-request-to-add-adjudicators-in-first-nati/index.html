Tribunal releases reasons for rejecting Canada's request to add adjudicators in First Nations child welfare case

Call #1Call #3

News

On November 3, 2026, Canadian Human Rights Tribunal Chairperson Jennifer Khurana issued reasons in 2026 CHRT 123 dismissing Canada's request to add two adjudicators to the long-running First Nations child welfare and Jordan's Principle proceeding. The dismissal had first been communicated to the parties on August 18. The ruling holds that the remaining member may continue the inquiry and that adding members at this stage would risk delay and added complexity.

Education

TRC Call #1 concerns reducing the number of Indigenous children in care and Call #3 calls on all levels of government to fully implement Jordan's Principle. This ruling concerns the procedure and continuity of a human rights proceeding relevant to those goals; it is not a finding that either Call has been completed, nor a new ruling on whether current services comply.

Accountability

The Tribunal noted that the parties' timetable includes final oral submissions in March 2027. The decision rejects Canada's panel-composition request, but does not resolve outstanding long-term reform, funding, or Jordan's Principle compliance issues. Those outcomes require separate evidence and continued monitoring.
