Canada raises its contribution to the 2028 North American Indigenous Games to $6.2 million

Call #88

News

At the official launch of the 2028 North American Indigenous Games in Winnipeg on November 19, 2026, Secretary of State (Sport) Adam van Koeverden announced an extra $1.3 million in federal support for the Games, bringing Canada's total to $6.2 million. The Games are hosted by Norway House Cree Nation and the Southern Chiefs' Organization and are set for July 9 to 23, 2028, in Winnipeg and communities across Manitoba, with about 5,250 participants from up to 700 Indigenous nations expected.

Context

TRC Call #88 asks all levels of government to support long-term Indigenous athlete development and continued support for the North American Indigenous Games, including funding to host them and for team preparation and travel. Canadian Heritage also said it is providing nearly $14.5 million in 2026-27 through the Sport for Social Development in Indigenous Communities program, and that Budget 2026 proposes $16 million over four years for Indigenous sport leadership and participation. Indigenous Watchdog lists Call #88 as in progress.

Accountability

The Games funding is a commitment for an event still more than a year and a half away; the money has not all been spent. The Budget 2026 measures are proposals that still need parliamentary approval. Call #88 also asks for support for provincial and territorial team preparation and travel, which this announcement does not set out in detail. Watch for the host province's contribution and for funding for athlete development beyond a single Games.
