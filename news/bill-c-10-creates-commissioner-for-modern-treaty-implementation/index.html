Bill C-10 receives Royal Assent, creating an independent Commissioner for Modern Treaty Implementation

Call #45

News

Crown-Indigenous Relations and Northern Affairs Canada announced that Bill C-10 received Royal Assent on November 19, 2026. The law creates a Commissioner for Modern Treaty Implementation, an independent Agent of Parliament comparable to the Auditor General. The Commissioner will review and audit how federal departments carry out Modern Treaty commitments, including related self-government agreements, and report directly to Parliament. Canada is currently implementing 27 Modern Treaties covering more than 40 per cent of the country's land mass.

Context

TRC Call #45 asks the federal government to issue a Royal Proclamation of Reconciliation, which would include renewing or establishing Treaty relationships based on mutual recognition, respect and shared responsibility. Independent oversight of whether treaty promises are kept is relevant to that relationship, but it is not the Proclamation the Call asks for, and it is not evidence that Call #45 has been completed.

Accountability

The government's release does not say when the office will open. Modern Treaty partners must be consulted on the appointment, which is for a seven-year term. The Land Claims Agreements Coalition said the real measure of success will be whether commitments are actually carried out. Watch for the appointment, the office's first reports and how federal departments respond to its findings.
