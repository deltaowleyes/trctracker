Dorothy Myo named Canada's second Commissioner of Indigenous Languages

Call #15

News

The federal government has appointed Dorothy Myo as Commissioner of Indigenous Languages for a five-year term, replacing interim commissioner Candice St-Aubin, The Canadian Press reported on November 11, 2026. A cabinet decision says she was selected after consultations with Indigenous governments and organizations. Myo, raised in Saulteaux First Nation in Saskatchewan, is the second person to hold the permanent role.

Context

TRC Call #15 asked the federal government to appoint an Indigenous languages commissioner to help promote Indigenous languages and report on the adequacy of federal funding. The office was created under the Indigenous Languages Act of 2019, and the first commissioner was appointed in 2021. Indigenous Watchdog lists Call #15 as complete.

Accountability

Myo takes over an office that has faced reported concerns from former staff about its operations and financial management, and Canadian Heritage has launched a financial audit. The completed Call created the office; whether it is effective is a separate question. Watch for the audit findings and the commissioner's reports on funding adequacy.
